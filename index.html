A real-time journey through a physically inspired black hole.

THE SHADOW
The dark region is the apparent black hole shadow. It is larger than the event horizon itself.
THE ACCRETION DISK
Orbiting matter heats up and radiates. The approaching side appears brighter through an artistic approximation of Doppler boosting.
GRAVITATIONAL LENSING
Curved light paths reveal the far side of the disk. This scene traces rays through an approximate, non-rotating Schwarzschild model.
THE PHOTON RING
Light passing near the photon sphere can circle the black hole, forming narrow images around its shadow. This is distinct from the event horizon.

A visualization, not a scientific instrument. Emission, colors and scale are art-directed. The interior is an artistic interpretation; the optional audio is a synthesized cinematic soundtrack.
